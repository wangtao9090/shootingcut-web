One recording → repeated shapes

Reframe Landscape Shooting Video for Social Formats

One landscape stage recording can be tracked on your Apple device and exported repeatedly for different portrait, square, or landscape publishing shapes without re-recording the stage.

Updated: 2026-07-29

Reframe changes the output frame. Track follows a selected person inside supported cropped ratios. Keep Source, non-tracked 16:9, and Split Sync’s layout-defined canvas separate from the normal Track workflow so the chosen shape and resolution stay within the real export ceiling.

Truthful output matrix

Match the publishing shape to the real output path

Vertical short video 9:16 Tracked crop 1080-pixel short edge; may downscale to 720p
Portrait feed 3:4, 4:5, or 6:7 Tracked crop 1080-pixel short edge; may downscale to 720p

Landscape video Source or 16:9 Source does not crop; 16:9 is not a Track ratio General choices depend on source and workflow ceiling

Do not combine the lists mechanically. General export choices may show Original, 4K, 1080p, and 720p when the selected source and workflow permit them. Tracked crops are not tracked 4K outputs.

Mode boundary

Use Reframe in the workflows that support it

Auto Trim, Merge, and Stage Mix support reframing. Auto Trim and Merge also support manual crop when a fixed composition is more useful than a person-following path.

Split Sync is different: its Full Screen, Dual View: Center HUD, Dual View: Top HUD, and Side by Side layouts control the canvas. Do not treat it as an arbitrary ratio-and-resolution Track workflow.

Six-step workflow

Track once, review every format

  1. Start in a supported mode. Open the landscape recording in Auto Trim, Merge, or Stage Mix, depending on the source workflow.
  2. Choose a tracked crop ratio. Select 9:16, 3:4, 4:5, 6:7, or 1:1 when you want Track to follow a person inside a cropped frame.
  3. Select and track the shooter. Choose the target person and let Shooting Cut calculate the tracking path on the Apple device.
  4. Review the tracked frame. Preview the full stage, check the crop during movement, and retrack or adjust the result when needed.
  5. Export within the tracked ceiling. Export the tracked crop with a 1080-pixel short edge or downscale it to 720p.
  6. Return for another shape. Return to the same edit, choose another supported ratio, review that composition, and export again without re-recording the stage.
Processing and access

Tracking runs on device, while mode access still applies

Person tracking is part of Shooting Cut’s on-device core media workflow. Original footage is not uploaded to a Shooting Cut media-processing server for tracking or reframing. Apple storage choices and uploads you initiate have separate network boundaries described in the on-device data-flow guide and Privacy Policy.

Auto Trim is free, and a free Auto Trim export includes the Shooting Cut watermark and logo intro card. Pro removes the watermark, enables custom intro cards, and unlocks Stage Mix. This guide does not add an unverified entitlement claim for Reframe or Merge.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Which ratios support person tracking?

Track supports cropped 9:16, 3:4, 4:5, 6:7, and 1:1 outputs. Source keeps the source frame, while 16:9 is a separate non-tracked ratio.

Can a tracked crop export in 4K?

No. Tracked crops use a 1080-pixel short edge and may be downscaled to 720p. General Original, 4K, 1080p, and 720p choices appear only when the source and selected workflow permit them.

Which editing modes support Reframe and Track?

Auto Trim, Merge, and Stage Mix support reframing. Auto Trim and Merge also support manual crop. Split Sync uses a layout-defined canvas instead of the normal Track ratio workflow.

Can I export more than one format from the same recording?

Yes. Return to the same edit, choose another supported shape, review its composition, and export again without re-recording the stage.
