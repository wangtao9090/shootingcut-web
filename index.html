Features

From Raw Stage Clips to a Finished Match Video

Use one purpose-built workflow to prepare a stage, synchronize camera angles, assemble a complete match, add performance context, reframe for social formats, and export.

✂️

Auto Trim (FREE)

Auto Trim takes exactly 1 video and cuts the dead air before and after your stage with customizable padding. Auto Trim and score import are free; free Auto Trim exports include the Shooting Cut watermark and logo intro card.

🎞️

Full Match Merge (PRO)

Arrange up to 20 sequential stage clips in competition order and export one Full Match video while preserving each clip's score association. The Stages Confirmation workbench shows the whole running order in one place so you can fix a stage's position, source, and intro card before the render starts.

🔄

Split Sync (PRO)

Split Sync takes exactly 2 videos and locks both angles into timeline alignment using the timer beep. Choose a synchronized layout for your POV and follow camera.

🎬

Stage Mix (PRO)

Stage Mix takes 2–3 videos labeled POV, Follow, or Static. Automatic movement-aware switching builds the cut, and manual override keeps every transition under your control.

🧵

Strings Mode (PRO)

Built for classifiers, Standards stages, and Steel Challenge. Strings Mode finds every string in a run, cuts the dead time between them, and timer-aligns multi-camera footage string by string into a split-screen or Stage Mix layout.

🎯

Reframe and Track (FREE IN AUTO TRIM)

Use Reframe and Track for free in Auto Trim to create a tracked crop of 9:16, 3:4, 4:5, 6:7, or 1:1. Source keeps the source frame, while 16:9 is a separate non-tracked option. Merge and Stage Mix also support Reframe within their Pro editing modes.

📊

Timing, Scores, and Results (FREE)

Import supported PractiScore, ESS, HDP, Shoot'n Score It, and IDPA results for free. When PractiScore supplies official per-shot records, align available times, splits, shot count, and score context to an Auto Trim video at no cost.

🎵

Reviewed Audio Analysis (FREE IN AUTO TRIM)

Analyze timer beeps and gunshot impulses for free in Auto Trim to propose trim points and timing data. Review detected markers when AGC, neighboring shots, echo, or weak beeps affect the recording. The waveform has two zoom levels: a whole-run view for finding the stage window, and a close view for moving a single marker.

🗂️

Batch Export on Mac (PRO)

Use Pro on macOS to render several prepared match edits as separate outputs. Batch export does not combine stages; Merge creates one Full Match video.

📱

Native on All Apple Devices

One subscription covers all of the subscriber's Apple devices. Use the desktop workflow on Mac or the purpose-built touch experience on iPhone and iPad.

🔒

Privacy-Aware Processing

Core audio/video analysis and person tracking run on device. Raw footage is not uploaded to a Shooting Cut media-processing server for editing or analysis, and there is no cross-app advertising tracking. See our Privacy Policy for iCloud, subscription, telemetry, score import, and user-initiated upload details.

🚀

Export and User-Initiated Sharing (FREE IN AUTO TRIM) (PRO: MULTI-PLATFORM)

Export a finished Auto Trim video to your device and use the system share sheet for free. You can also choose one supported sharing destination at a time; Pro enables multi-platform sharing in one workflow. Platform authorization is separate.

🎞️

Intro Title Cards (FREE DEFAULT) (PRO: CUSTOM)

Free Auto Trim exports include the Shooting Cut logo intro card. Pro enables custom intro cards with match details, shooter information, and your own logo. In a Full Match Merge, every stage can carry its own intro card, so a viewer knows which stage they are watching without you cutting title slides by hand.

💧

Custom Watermarks (PRO)

Pro can remove the Shooting Cut watermark or replace it with custom text, a logo, or an image, and you can place more than one custom watermark in the same export. Free Auto Trim exports retain the Shooting Cut watermark.

🧲

Movable Overlays (PRO)

Drag the watermark, score card, timer, and rolling shot list to wherever the frame has room. Move an overlay off the target array, out of the vertical crop, or away from a platform's caption bar instead of accepting a fixed corner.

💬

Subtitle Content Control

Decide what the performance subtitle says. Switch the timer, best split, first shot, rolling shot list, time column, and split column on or off one by one, and choose how many shot rows stay on screen — a full data readout for a review edit, or a bare timer for a social clip.

Five Modes for Every Situation

Auto Trim (FREE)

Exactly 1 video

Merge (PRO)

Up to 20 videos

Split Sync (PRO)

Exactly 2 videos

Lock exactly two angles in sync using the timer beep. Stack both views top and bottom in Full Screen, add a Center or Top HUD, or place them Side by Side. Multi-angle top-and-bottom and HUD layouts support 50/50, 40/60, and 30/70 pane ratios.

Best for: dual-angle comparison

2–3 videos

One run, many strings

Best for: classifiers, Standards, Steel Challenge

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Shooting Cut free?

Shooting Cut is free to download. Auto Trim, its audio analysis, Reframe and Track, supported result import, timing and score overlays, standard export, and one-destination user-initiated sharing are free. Free Auto Trim exports include the Shooting Cut watermark and logo intro card. Pro unlocks Merge, Split Sync, Stage Mix, and Strings Mode, removes the watermark, and adds custom intro cards, movable overlays, multi-platform sharing, and macOS batch export. One subscription covers all of the subscriber's Apple devices. No Shooting Cut account or sign-up is required. Check the App Store listing for current subscription pricing in your region.

Which editing mode fits my footage?

Use Auto Trim for exactly 1 video, Merge for up to 20 videos, Split Sync for exactly 2 videos, or Stage Mix for 2–3 videos labeled POV, Follow, or Static. Stage Mix uses automatic movement-aware switching and supports manual override.

What cameras work with Shooting Cut?

Any camera that records video with audio — GoPro, DJI Action, Insta360, iPhone, or any other camera. As long as the timer beep and gunshots are audible in the video audio, Shooting Cut can detect and analyze them. For best results, ensure the microphone isn't blocked or muffled.

Is my video data safe?

Core audio/video analysis and person tracking run on device. Raw footage is not uploaded to a Shooting Cut media-processing server for editing or analysis, and there is no cross-app advertising tracking. See our Privacy Policy for iCloud, subscription, telemetry, score import, and user-initiated upload details.
